# TextSigns

![TextSigns logo](src/main/resources/Common/UI/leniad_TextSigns.png "TextSigns")

A Hytale server plugin that lets players write on signs. The text is drawn on the face of the sign, so every player can read it just by looking at the sign.

This is a fork of [TextSigns by Daniel Novaes Dias](https://github.com/DanielNovaesDias/TextSigns), ported to Hytale 0.7 and changed to draw text on the sign itself instead of showing it in a HUD popup.

## Requirements

- A Hytale server running 0.7.x.
- No other plugins are needed.

## Installing

1. Download `TextSigns-<version>.jar` from the releases page, or build it yourself (see below).
2. Put the jar in the server's `mods` folder.
3. Restart the server.

## Using signs

- Place one of the supported signs, then use it (the interact key) to open the editor.
- Type your text. It appears on the sign as you type, as a preview.
- Press Enter or click Save to keep the text. Press Esc or Back to discard your changes.
- Tick Lock Sign to stop anyone changing the text. Only the player who placed the sign can lock or unlock it. Signs placed by world generation can be locked by anyone.
- Breaking a sign removes its text.

Text wraps between words and shrinks to fit the sign, using up to five lines. Hanging and standing signs show the text on both sides; wall signs show it on the front only.

## Supported signs

Signs are made at the Builder's Workbench from the material shown. Where a sign needs planks, turn the logs into planks at the workbench first.

| Sign | Made from |
|---|---|
| Simple Wooden Sign | Hardwood: Oak, Ash, Apple or Fir logs, or Hardwood planks |
| Tavern Sign | Darkwood: Cedar logs or Darkwood planks |
| Lumberjack Sign | Redwood: Redwood, Maple or Blue Fig logs, or Redwood planks |
| Crude Sign | Softwood or Lightwood planks, made from Aspen, Banyan, Beech, Birch, Camphor, Gumboab or Sadwillow logs |
| Ancient Sign | Blackwood or Deadwood planks, made from Burnt, Fire or Petrified logs |
| Sandswept Sign | Drywood or Goldenwood planks, made from Dry, Bottletree, Amber, Palm Tree, Sallow or Wild Wisteria logs |
| Frozen Castle Sign | Frostwood logs |
| Dark Temple Sign | Shale or Shale Cobble |
| Overgrown Temple Sign | Quartzite or Quartzite Cobble |
| Wind Temple Sign | Sandstone or Sandstone Cobble |
| Feran Sign | Not craftable |
| Lost Civilization Sign | Not craftable |
| Kweebec Sign | Not craftable |

These are the vanilla recipes; the plugin doesn't change them. Logs that aren't listed, such as Azure, don't make any sign.

### Other vanilla signs

These signs can be crafted in vanilla Hytale but can't be written on yet:

| Sign | Made from |
|---|---|
| Goblin Sign | 15 Scraps and 5 planks of any wood, at the Goblin Tinkerer's Workbench once it is powered and its Boiler is repaired |
| Neon Sign - Fruit | 1 Darkwood - Half Slab, 1 Iron Ingot and 1 Iron Chain - Small, at the Architect's Workbench (makes 2) |
| Neon Sign - Tea | 1 Darkwood - Half Slab, 1 Iron Ingot and 1 Iron Chain - Small, at the Architect's Workbench (makes 2) |

The Under Construction sign and the prototype signs can't be crafted or written on.

## Limitations

- Signs show printable ASCII characters only. Any other character is shown as `?`.
- Text is limited to 200 characters, and text that doesn't fit in five lines is cut off.
- Each character on a sign is a small entity, so a sign full of text adds a few hundred entities to its chunk.

## Building

You need JDK 25 and a copy of the Hytale 0.7 server jar.

1. Put the server jar at the root of the repository as `HytaleServer.jar`. It isn't committed.
2. Run `./gradlew build`.
3. The plugin is written to `build/libs/`.

The glyph textures, glyph metrics and glyph models are generated by a Node.js script in [`tools/glyphs`](tools/glyphs/README.md). The generated files are committed, so you only need to run it after changing a font or ink colour.
